CRITICAL ASSIGNMENT RULE - CONTEXTUAL RELATIONSHIP ANALYSIS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Determine article assignment to TARGET_SR through SYNTACTIC and SEMANTIC analysis:

PRIMARY INDICATORS (Strong Evidence):

1. DIRECT MARKING - Acronym immediately follows article:
   ✓ "Art. 32 und 58 FHG" → Art. 32, 58 belong to SR 611.0
   ✓ "Artikel 12 Absatz 4 FHG" → Art. 12 Abs. 4 belongs to SR 611.0

2. PREPOSITIONAL ATTRIBUTION - Preposition explicitly links article to source:
   ✓ "nach Art. 20 Abs. 2 der Publikationsverordnung vom 7. Okt. 2015 (SR 170.512.1)"
   → Art. 20 Abs. 2 belongs to SR 170.512.1 (preposition "der" links to document)
   
   ✓ "gemäss Art. 146 Abs. 4 ParlG SR 171.10"
   → Art. 146 Abs. 4 belongs to SR 171.10 (same sentence structure)

3. PARENTHETICAL CITATION - Article in parentheses adjacent to SR reference:
   ✓ "(Art. 18 Abs. 3)" appearing in subheading with "(Art. 29 FHG)"
   → Analyze if both belong to FHG or if separated

SECONDARY INDICATORS (Contextual Clues):

4. CLAUSE DEPENDENCY - Article mentioned in dependent clause modifying SR citation:
   ✓ "gestützt auf das Finanzhaushaltgesetz (FHG), insbesondere Artikel 32"
   → Art. 32 likely belongs to FHG (dependent on main clause)

5. PROXIMITY WITHIN SENTENCE - Article and SR reference in same sentence:
   - If SR link appears in sentence, analyze if article syntactically relates to it
   - Check for connecting words: "insbesondere", "namentlich", "sowie"
   - Determine if article is object of same verb/preposition as SR document

SELF-REFERENCE INDICATORS (DO NOT assign to TARGET_SR):

1. ISOLATED ARTICLE MENTIONS - No SR document mentioned nearby:
   ✗ "nach den Artikeln 18 und 21" (no SR context in sentence)
   ✗ "gemäss Art. 54" (standalone reference)
   
2. SEPARATE SYNTACTIC STRUCTURES - Article in independent clause from SR:
   ✗ "(Art. 32 FHG) ... die Weisungen nach den Artikeln 18 und 21"
   → "Artikeln 18 und 21" in separate phrase = self-reference

3. REGULATORY LANGUAGE - Internal document structure references:
   ✗ "Der Bundesrat erlässt Weisungen zu den Artikeln 4–6"
   → Refers to articles within current document's structure

ANALYSIS PROCEDURE:

For each article number found:
Step 1: Identify the syntactic boundaries (clause, phrase, sentence)
Step 2: Locate any SR references within the same syntactic unit
Step 3: Analyze grammatical relationship:
   - Is article the object of a preposition that also governs the SR?
   - Is article in apposition to the SR reference?
   - Does a connector explicitly link them?
Step 4: If no grammatical link exists → Self-reference
Step 5: If grammatical link exists → Assign to TARGET_SR

EXAMPLE ANALYSIS:

Input: "(Art. 32 und 58 FHG) ... die Grundsätze nach Artikel 12 Absatz 4 FHG sowie die Weisungen und Anforderungen nach den Artikeln 18 und 21"

Analysis:
• "(Art. 32 und 58 FHG)" - Direct marking → Assign to SR 611.0: Art. 32, 58
• "nach Artikel 12 Absatz 4 FHG" - Direct marking → Assign to SR 611.0: Art. 12 Abs. 4
• "nach den Artikeln 18 und 21" - No SR reference in phrase, separate prepositional phrase → Self-reference

Result: Assign to SR 611.0: Art. 32, Art. 58, Art. 12 Abs. 4 only

EDGE CASE - Ambiguous Syntax:
If syntactic relationship is unclear, DEFAULT to self-reference unless explicit marker present.
